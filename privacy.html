Privacy Policy

Aurelia: Zodiac Affirmations · Last updated September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Aurelia: Zodiac Affirmations ("Aurelia," "the app," "we," "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Aurelia does not require an account or sign-in.

Information we store

Aurelia stores the following on your device only, using standard iOS local storage (shared between the app and its home screen widget via an App Group so your widget can show your content):

  • Your selected zodiac sign and app preferences (theme, notification schedule, affirmation phrasing style)
  • Affirmations you've saved or written yourself
  • Your streak and progress data
  • An optional first name, if you choose to enter one during onboarding

None of this information is transmitted to us or to any server we control. It stays on your device (and syncs only to your own iCloud backup, if you have iOS device backup enabled — Aurelia does not manage or access iCloud directly).

Subscriptions and payment

Aurelia offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details — Apple handles all billing. Subscription status is managed through our paywall provider, Superwall, which helps us display pricing and manage entitlement to premium features. See Superwall's own privacy policy for details on their processing.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Aurelia schedules local reminders on your device using Apple's notification framework. These are scheduled locally and are not sent from a remote server.

Analytics

We use Superwall to understand paywall performance (e.g. which subscription plan is selected, whether a purchase completed). This data is not linked to your name or used for third-party advertising, and Aurelia does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or any cross-app tracking.

Children's privacy

Aurelia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear all locally stored data at any time by deleting the app. You can disable notifications in iOS Settings or within Aurelia's in-app notification settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
