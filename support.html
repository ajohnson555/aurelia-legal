Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?
Open Aurelia and follow the onboarding prompts — you'll see a "Restore" link on the subscription screen. Tap it and sign in with the Apple ID you originally subscribed with.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone to view, change, or cancel.

How do I change my notification schedule?
Open the app, tap the gear icon, then Notifications, to adjust your reminder times.
